# Source contracts and review limits

1. `run.py` sets exact/recipe108, eps default .1, clears STATS, and adds a terminal-cell wrapper. The cell counter itself increments in `_cell_error`. `PAT` uses direct floating recurrence at the mean of cone rays only in exact mode. `_margin_split` still relies on floating halfspace/rank tolerances; source `exact=True` means no approximate gate-freezing, not exact arithmetic.
2. Mean of every generating ray with strictly positive coefficients is in the relative interior of a valid polyhedral cone. Full dimensionality and correct facet representation make it interior in ambient space. Under exact evaluation, gate signs then identify the activation history, apart from identically zero forms whose OFF convention must agree. Binary mean/recurrence rounding can violate the premise. A fixed two-ray dyadic control will demonstrate this possibility without asserting it occurs in any saved trained run.
3. Distinct activation histories need not be distinct affine maps. This review uses the author's activation-region interpretation, not a minimal linear-region claim.
4. `summary.py` reads local result files first. For H8T4 it falls back to `estT_fix/rescore/<name>.json` for missing modes. The frozen dependencies must bind this exact source choice, never interchange independent reference versions.
5. Published ratio uses continuous N=ops/fpe_flops, with fpe_flops=6000*flops(H,T)/5760 and flops=2*T*H*(H+2). We will reproduce that convention and separately show the DRI T196 2*ops/c_s restatement as conditional on counter completeness, not as a certified FLOP ceiling.
6. Region sample counts come from 200k and2M random inputs with seed5. They are finite floating pattern counts; no independent sampling or real-network certificate is available from saved integers alone. Stored MC proportions permit inferred integer hits, not recovery of raw RNG states or independent samples.
7. Original source history/folder publication remains a separate request. The review snapshot records original paths/ref/blob and bytes, and is shared under its own ownership; no author source is modified and no foreign branch is merged wholesale.
