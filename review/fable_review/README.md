## A. Critical review (ranked by how much it would change conclusions)

**A1. The exact-route verdict uses the wrong precision target, and rests on one clean growth factor.** SCALING_OBSTRUCTIONS §3.4 and T222 judge mass-ordered enumeration by N_1e-5. But a deductive method beats sampling only if its error is below 16/sqrt(C) at *its own* cost. A region-by-region method at N regions with d=10 cone masses costs >= ~1e6 FLOPs/region (d=5 is already 5e4-1.7e5 per simplex, ~6 simplices/leaf, T194 "T206"); at N=1e10 that is >=1e16 FLOPs, where sampling's SE is 1.6e-7. So the uncovered remainder must be bounded to ~1e-7, not 1e-5, and the deep decades are the ones whose growth rises with k (1.12/1.29/1.48 at k=3/4/5). This makes the route *deader* than stated. In the other direction, "confirmed for k<=4" is one unsaturated step (3->4, x16.1; 2->3 is capped since N/regions=1 at k=2, as T218 Part 2 notes) plus the unverified 4->5 (x14.9), and the factor is *falling* ~8%/step; six-step extrapolations from this are weak either way (3e10 vs 2e11). Consequences: (i) state the requirement as "the full count with a remainder <1e-7"; (ii) note that an *exact* method satisfies "MSE <= sampling at equal compute" trivially at its own cost, so feasibility is the only question, hinging on g(5->6), g(6->7) and the d=10 mass cost. k=6 masses (2.3e7 regions; orth6 exists) are feasible on the GPU already used and were not computed.

**A2. "Errors live only near ties or in the blind region" is overstated.** T239's follow-up table (shatter_test, (16,4) zoo0) gives error by stratum: d>=0.05 non-blind holds (1.6+1.3+0.086)e-4 of 2.13e-3 = **14%** of the error, in *mixed* leaves. At T=10 on the target, T241 Phase D says the far-from-tie 80.4% of mass has error rate 0.83%, i.e. 0.67 pp = **14%** of 4.7 pp. And the gap-binned error rate decays smoothly (.434/.272/.143/.062/.030/.014/.011 by m2-m3 bin, T241 Phase D), with no band edge. Only *fully wrong* leaves are confined to d<0.05 or blind. A method that treats a thin band and leaves the "far" part to a bound has an uncovered 0.67 pp, 40x the sampling SE at 1e10 FLOPs. The report's §5 and the brief's "errors live only near ties" should read "~86% of the error is within gap 0.05 of a tie or blind; the rest is a soft tail".

**A3. The noise regime is under-weighted.** Every exploitable structure found (tie-wall concentration, blind region = 60% of the face second moment, the 1.1-1.3x) is a *clean-model* fact. On the sigma 0.01 copy the blind share is 8% and 65% of the tail sits at u>0.5 (T218); abs sigma 0.05 drops accuracy to 0.62-0.83 with bulk errors (gpu_cost_law noisy table). §3.6's "noise does not change this" is about region *counts* (0.94-1.21x), the wrong invariant: what changes is where the error is. ARC's noise level is unknown (H-E4); its M_4,3 panel used abs 0.05, which makes M_16,10 a 19%-accuracy model. The panel is a different problem from the clean target, and no tie-band method addresses it. This belongs at the top of §5.

**A4. "Region count" is the right obstruction only for region-by-region methods; the report partly conflates three obstructions.** (i) Enumeration: count (correct). (ii) Face quadrature (sci_tao class): the obstruction is the *kink density* of the band integral F on an 8-D face. Fixed-node cubature on a function with s bounded derivatives in d dimensions has worst-case rate N^(-s/d); with kinks (s~1) and d=8 it cannot beat N^(-1/2). This explains H-E11 (507x at T=3 where F has few pieces, 1.6x at T=10) without "heavy tails" as a primitive, and predicts that *no* fixed-node face rule wins at T=10 unless d_eff<=2 or F is smoothed by an exact identity. (iii) Perturbative/probe methods: the obstruction is the exponent alpha in bias ~ C^(-alpha); only alpha>1/2 can win. Probe v2->v4 (FINDINGS §5) had bias 0.74->0.59->0.25 pp at 32k->80k->167k evals, alpha~1 over three points: the only family with a winning *rate*, abandoned for its prefactor and one blow-up. The project never measures alpha; it compares absolute MSE at one or two budgets. Reframing by rate changes which families deserve effort.

**A5. T232's control is not active-fraction matched.** Random draws are 0.32-0.70 active vs 0.77 trained (random_control table); more active gates means more cutting hyperplanes, so "same count" may hide "fewer than an equally active random net". Cheap to fix. DRI_HANDBOOK §7 bullet 4 ("trained nets use a tiny fraction of possible regions") is refuted by T232 and should be struck.

**A6. Certification is stricter than ARC's bar.** T195 phase 2: the certified bound B is 10-100x the actual pruning error (cap tau 1e-5 at (8,5): ratio_B 6e6, ratio_act 0.019). T206 calls its oracle skip an upper bound because "a real method must bound each cell"; ARC requires empirical MSE on a panel, not certificates. Moot where savings are constant-factor, but as a design rule it shuts doors.

**A7. Smaller points.** (a) The k=6 "x30.6" is cells, not regions, from 245/3811 subtrees with no variance reported. (b) The 16-scramble MSE in tie_blind is RQMC: a sampling MSE by ARC's definition, so "best deterministic 1.1-1.3x" is a quadrature number only in the single-run sense. (c) estT ops undercount 1.37x nominal and kappa in [1,2] (T196); the (16,4) "pass" panel is 4 copies of seed 0 at rel 0.005, below directive 7. (d) T241: the review's corrections (implementation-dependent .955-.966; fragile accuracy match; 8.37 not 4.32, and mode damage not specific vs random subspaces; n9 = its W_hh row) are now adopted in e3d0a0de and §8, and the H-M3 note is right. The new recurrent variant is the most useful Q4 fact in T241: formula states injected at t0 keep >=.99 agreement through t0=7 and fail over the last 2-3 steps (.983/.978/.955), with the hybrid's *accuracy* rising monotonically .9525 -> .9570 (t0=9). That is H-E10 in its cleanest form: every surrogate of the trained net is a cleaner algorithm, so any reference-network or formula-based estimator carries a positive bias of 0.1-0.8 pp, 10-50x the bar, and the sign is not certified. §8's reading ("understood except exactly where accuracy is decided") is correct; its Q4 corollary should be stated: the account cannot serve as an estimator, and the error lives in the last 2-3 steps' state deviations (1.7-2.9% relative) hitting the near-tie readout. (e) T217's "kill" is by a straw-man modal rule; its real finding (exp H(G|E) grows x1.9/step vs x27 for regions) is dead only because error sits in the atypical tail. (f) Pre-registration and held-out discipline are good; no leakage found.

## B. First principles

Accuracy = 1 - mu(E), E a union of polyhedral cones in R^10, mu = solid angle on S^9 (homogeneity is fully used). Exchangeability buys only the label side: 90 equiprobable (p1,p2) classes and exact Renyi/record probabilities; the network is not permutation-equivariant. Near a wall x_a=x_b the co-area identity err = sum Z E_face[F] is exact and in use; the whole difficulty is the 8-D face integral of a rough F.

Necessary conditions for a deductive winner, given the evidence: it must neither visit regions (x25/step) nor place fixed nodes on a kinked 8-D function (rate). That leaves four shapes: (a) exact enumeration (wins at its own cost if feasible; A1); (b) an exact identity that makes the integrand smooth, e.g. conditional flip probability given a skeleton, Phi(-mu/sigma)(skeleton), is smooth and 3-D, but computing the conditional law of the low-input contribution deterministically is the 7-D problem again (E31 dead-verified on cost vs leverage; the oracle statistical linearisation had bias ~0, which says the *shape* is right); (c) an expansion in a small parameter (gain mismatch 1.8%, 0.4% flip effect per low input) whose remainder is *measured* on the panel, not certified (A6), with alpha>1/2; (d) dimension reduction by exact integration over directions with few pieces, which fails here (~250 pieces per great circle, E22; last-step-only gives 1.5x).

Markov structure: backward induction V_t(h_t, state) smooths by one Gaussian convolution per step, but h_t carries ~(t+1) input dimensions, so V_9 is as rough as the problem; the DP collapses only if the network forgets, and the state it does not forget is exactly the error (H-E10, E13/E27; T241's recurrent variant puts that residue in the last 2-3 steps). Not yet exploited: a basis for h in which the margin does not cancel (H-E9: eigenmode basis untested); the active-fraction control; the exponent alpha.

## C. Ideas (with the failure each avoids, the decisive test, and the risk)

1. **Finish the exact route's decision with k=6 full masses** (target prefix, 2.3e7 regions, d=6 on the A100; hours). Avoids: extrapolating from one clean step. Test: g_1e-5 and g_1e-7 for 5->6; mass-rank slope. Stop rule: g_1e-7 >= 12 => exact route dead for good; <= 7 and falling => fund (16,6) zoo0 final leaves (~3e13 ops) to measure error-layer growth and per-region mass cost at d=6 as a d=10 proxy. Risk: the answer is "12", which is likely; but it is the one number that could reopen a route with a clean win condition.

2. **Rate (alpha) measurement for every deterministic family** (sci_tao fixed rule per budget, probe v2-v5, FORM/SORM with 2x/4x design points, mass-truncated enumeration at T=4/5). Avoids judging by prefactor at one budget. Test: 4 budgets spanning 16x; alpha<=0.5 closes a family, alpha>0.5 earns prefactor work. Days, existing code. Risk: alpha drifts with budget (E33); report with CIs.

3. **Probe series pushed to order 3-4 with robust multi-point probes.** Avoids: the prefactor problem of v4 by riding alpha~1 (if real). Test: v5 on orig + the 30 LB3 models; the series wins at ~1e11 FLOPs only if bias halves per cost doubling for ~5 more orders. Stop if v5's bias is >0.6x v4's or any copy blows up >1 pp. Risk: high; v4 failed by -7.7 pp on one copy, and "bias halves per order" is three points of non-systematic corrections. Probably dead, but cheap to kill properly.

4. **Linear-relaxation (CROWN/zonotope) certification tree on S^9 instead of E19's intervals.** Avoids: interval blow-up (5e5x). Test at (16,4) zoo0 where leaves are known: certified mass vs number of caps, vs intervals; then (16,5). Stop if <90% of the d>=0.1 mass certifies with <1e4 caps at T=4. Risk: 10 recurrent steps compound any relaxation; and certifying the far 80% leaves the near-tie 20% (86% of error) unresolved, so deductively it is dead and as a hybrid it caps at ~8x variance reduction (0.196^2 x 0.18 x 0.82 / 0.0447).

5. **Reproduce ARC's estimator A on M_16,10** (rank classes x modal gate word x presumption of independence x exact cone volumes; E32: ARC called 2.4-10x worse "roughly match"). Avoids optimising for a bar ARC may not use. Test: LB3 scoring, target ratio <=10. Risk: this is rank_exact (E10, 37-450x) with other independence assumptions; probably 20-50x. Calibration for T203, not a win.

6. **Skeleton-conditional moments by gate-frozen mixture** (skeleton fixes most gates; Gaussian closure for the few unresolved low-input gates). Avoids enumerating low-input patterns. Status: R5's 10 variants killed E31 on cost (>=64-128 FPE/skeleton) vs leverage. Dead unless a cheaper closure appears.

7. **In-band-flip freezing as an approximate band identity.** Replace F by F0 computed with gates frozen at the band entry (no in-band flips), an exactly computable, far smoother integrand; measure the integrated bias F-F0 on the target and copies (T218 has the flip inventory: n5 steps 5-9, n3). Avoids: the kink density that kills face quadrature. Decisive test: if |E[F]-E[F0]| < 0.02 pp on 30 models, F0 is a candidate with alpha to be measured; E24's per-point linear-response ratio 0.95-3.7x predicts failure, but the *integrated* number was never reported. Risk: 27% of tail mass has >=5 in-band flips (T218); likely bias ~0.3 pp.

8. **Same-future merging at k=5 on the target** (cluster the 632k affine maps with a margin-based equivalence). Avoids: counting regions that cannot change any decision. Test: cluster count at a tolerance that provably cannot flip a future gate on the cone; need <=1/5 of regions to matter. Risk: T188 found no strict equivalences; approximate merging is the certificate problem again. Likely <2x.

9. **Exact x8,x9 marginalisation** (2-D polygon masses via bivariate normal CDF) on top of any skeleton method: one smoother dimension; E12/E16 show the last step alone gives ~1.5x. Low value.

10. **Reference network + joint residual law** (ARC's B), now with T241's recurrent hybrid (t0=7) as the best available reference: 99% agreement, so the residual integrand is nonzero on ~1% of inputs instead of 4.7%. As a sampling control variate that is at most ~4x; as a deductive method it still needs the residual integral, which is the original rough 10-D problem with a +0.22 pp offset (hybrid accuracy .9547 vs .9525). T37-P1: independent residual laws miss by 22-48 pp. Dead deductively.

## E. Ranking and odds

Top 3: **(1) k=6 masses + (16,6) zoo0 leaves** (the one route with a clean win condition; the extrapolation is the project's weakest load-bearing number). **(2) alpha measurements + ARC-A reproduction** (cheap; redefines progress; prepares T203 before learning ARC's method). **(3) Kink density along T and the integrated in-band-flip effect** (settles whether any analytic band treatment can reach 0.02 pp or whether the 8-D rough face integral is irreducible).

Odds: M_16,10 solved under ARC's strict rule (deterministic, no pseudorandom inputs, MSE <= plain sampling at equal compute, across 5 seeds and noise that moves accuracy by a few pp) by anyone within a year: **10-15%**; by this project with its current toolbox: **~5%**. Under ARC's demonstrated M_4,3 leniency (2-10x called "roughly match"): **40-50%**. That ARC's reported solution meets the strict bar: **25-35%**; more likely it is exact enumeration at large cost (feasible only if g falls to <=7/step, which k=6 would show) or an independence-style estimator within a lenient factor. For T203: ask ARC for noise levels, budgets, and whether low-discrepancy nodes count as sampling; without those, no comparison is interpretable.
