## Recoverability must be distinguished from direct retention

A missing explicit integer field does not mean every count is irrecoverable. Given declared N and sufficiently precise saved shares, one may be able to recover a unique compatible integer denominator under the original floating mean/rounding semantics. That requires a separately frozen integer-compatibility audit; multiplying an approximate share by N and silently rounding is not the present evidence. Rounded five-decimal matrix entries can leave several compatible hit counts. Full-precision matrix data may help with marginal hits, if its contents and source binding are independently verified.

Even exact marginal accuracy, agreement and all case denominators generally do not recover the full paired correctness table. Disagreement can include both-wrong/different-label rows as well as correct-to-wrong and wrong-to-correct rows. These have different contributions to accuracy-change variance. A new sampling confidence claim cannot be retrofitted merely from the README's generic standard error. Shared permutation donors and same-bank pattern selection are additional design qualifications, not errors corrected by more decimal places.

T251 and independent review d15ef043 already establish that the source's squared oblique projected norm is generally not variance removed. The current JSON retains the name energy_share and the same generating source comment; no retained cross term or full projector enables a removed-SSE reconstruction without further data/computation. The README itself flags ill-conditioned selected patterns. This inventory neither validates their numerical conditioning nor changes any saved ablation accuracy. It also does not infer that every mode is unreliable merely because the energy label is wrong.

Owner correction can preserve the existing diagnostic while naming it precisely and distinguishing signed/off-manifold, donor-conditioned interventions from mechanistic replacement. Publishing known retained diagnostics would be preferable to recomputation. Any new diagnostics or model replay require a separate scientific and resource scope, not authorization from this inventory.

## Scope of phases and claims

The README marks phase A done, phase B running, and phases C/D pending. The frozen subtree contains only phase-A scripts/results. Consequently no phase-B heldout regression or phase-C all-neuron replacement outcome is validated here. Large ablation effects are sensitivity to defined interventions; they do not alone prove irreducible neuron roles or a unique mechanism. Likewise, aggregate-result provenance gaps are not evidence that the reported scores are false.
