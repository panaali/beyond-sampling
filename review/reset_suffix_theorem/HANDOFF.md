exactly when the latest different mask is erased by a later common all-off
mask (identical words included). Resets suffice universally. The converse
excludes a finite polynomial exceptional set, using the fresh-input column
and a single tied all-ones W witness. Joint absolute continuity gives the
probability-one corollary; training, finite binary arithmetic and structural
weight laws do not inherit it automatically.

are distinct in T188. It does not prove anything about feasible-domain or
label-only equality, approximate merging, convex unions, mass or efficiency.

reviewed output-fingerprint hypotheses; they are not established here.
