Read: shared `ccr-6a61f25c-wn80ii` (TASKS Directives 6-8 + both amendments, INDEX T216-T281 and ledger, SCALING_OBSTRUCTIONS post-T263, FINDINGS, codex_astro_review, observer, stoch_margin, reliability READMEs), `claude/t194-width16-probe` (rate_alpha, rate_T), `claude/t241-neuron-account` (probe_v5, gap_formula, neuron_account), `claude/t218-tie-tail-labels` (lenient_bb, tie_tail_labels Part 3), `claude/hp1-scorer`, `claude/t192-gpu-cost-law` (hp1/, hp2/dev2), lit/web_arc (Hilton notes, S4, S7-S12). Arithmetic only; nothing run. Child results are unverified unless the INDEX says otherwise.

Jargon used below: **FPE** = one sampled forward pass (5948 FLOPs). **RMS** = root-mean-square error across the panel's models, in percentage points (pp). **Own-cost figure of merit** F = RMS^2 x C / p(1-p): the estimator's MSE divided by sampling's MSE at the estimator's own cost C; F <= 1 wins. **Skeleton** = the positions and values of the top-3 inputs; **low inputs** = ranks 4-10. **Frozen gates** = using one ReLU on/off pattern for a whole class of inputs.

## A. Audit

**A1. The arithmetic of the reframing is right; the floor is ours.** n* = p(1-p)/e^2 is correct: at p(1-p) ~ .074 (mean of a panel with mean accuracy .92, sd .03) sampling's RMS is .86 pp at 1k FPE, .43 at 4k, .21 at 16k, .054 at 256k. Two caveats. (a) The 1k "informative floor" is a project convention. ARC named no budget, and a weight-dependent estimator with 2 pp RMS at 100 FPE would formally match sampling at its own cost. The principled floor is "beat the prior": a constant at the panel mean has RMS ~3 pp, so n* ~ 80 FPE; the DRI's 1k is 12x stricter. Defensible (ARC wants informative estimates), but state it as ours and report the whole (cost, MSE) curve, as Amendment 2 already says. (b) Model-free precomputation (order-statistic laws, Renyi transforms, quadrature tables) is uncharged by LB3 convention. ARC's MSP post (S7) allows bounded "advice", and ARC's own M_4,3 accounting charged only its algorithm's operations, so this is probably fair, but a cheap-regime estimator that leans on large model-free tables is exposed if ARC charges them.

**A2. "Everything we tried was aimed at 16k-256k" is half true.** LB3 always scored each run at its own charged cost. So the DRI's own quoted "best mechanistic per group 37-130x" (Directive 8) already is the own-cost figure of merit, minimum over budgets. Restated at properly reported budgets (table below), deductive families sit at F ~ 100-400 on clean models and >= 1000 on weak copies. The reframing does not shrink the gap for existing methods; it says a *new* cheap method with ~.85 pp RMS at 1k FPE would pass, and that nobody built one because every deductive method spends >= 10k FPE resolving configurations.

**A3. The scoring rules are fair to ARC, with two notes.** Classing fixed Sobol nodes as inductive matches ARC's own words ("avoids any random or pseudorandom sampling", S7), not only our reading of Hilton. The strict-bar restatement (point ratio <= 1; "significant" needs upper CI <= 1) is forced by 6 clusters; HP2's 100 retrains will make "significant" testable. Making HP2 (retrains) primary is right: ARC said "random seed or maybe noise", and absolute sigma .05 turns M_16,10 into a 10-34% model (T276), which is not the target's regime. Note that DEV2's open truths are not yet on the branch (hp2/dev2 has weights and manifest only); the D-test below needs them.

**A4. Conclusions since round 1: mostly sound, three qualifiers.** (i) Probe family dead (verified, scoped): sound. The T271 knob (number of canonical arrangements) cannot remove the Jensen bias by construction, so alpha ~ 0 was expected; the kill rests on T274a's independent variants and the noisy-copy failure mechanism (107/720 triples with no threshold in range, missing rank >= 4 mode, fixed 8/9 blind rate). (ii) FORM/SORM "closed": right on copies (fixed +.5 to +2.4 pp offset), slightly over-firm on the target, where E17's own ladder shows +1.9 -> +.15 pp over 35k -> 2.48M (alpha ~ .6); T271's x16 span has CI [-.5, .9]. Irrelevant for the bar. (iii) Dimension factor refuted: yes (alpha_rmse .49-.67 at every T, rho .03). The residual ~.6 > .5 means sci_tao's ratio keeps improving slowly with budget (0.78 at 256k on clean zoo seeds, T277), still inductive. (iv) The T272 kill of in-band freezing (-7 to -21 pp) and E31's small aggregate (-.13 to -.45 pp) are consistent: freezing *and extrapolating across the band* fails; conditioning on an exact skeleton and linearising only in the low inputs nearly works on 3 models. Keep both facts; they point at the same design (C1).

**A5. The population changed and the mechanism knowledge did not.** The target (.953) sits near the top of 40 retrains (mean .92, sd .03). Two thirds of the HP2 panel are weaker models whose extra error is a model-specific soft tail: 19-45% of error at gap >= .05 on the zoo seeds versus 14% on the target (T269), including gross errors at gap >= .2 (zoo1: 1.8 pp). FINDINGS, T241 and every band diagnostic describe the target. Nothing in the repo says what makes a retrain weak. That is the first thing to measure (C6).

## B. The decisive question

**(i) 1k-4k FPE on retrains: can a deductive estimator beat sampling?** Bar: RMS <= .86 pp at 1k, .43 at 4k. In information terms: explain 92% of the seed-to-seed variance (3 pp -> .86) from the weights, deductively, with ~1000 forward passes of weight-dependent work.

*For.* (1) F is now the right lens, and the best deductive families are 100-400x off, not the 1e3-1e5x of the precision regime; F ~ e^2 C, so a 3x bias cut plus a 10-40x cost cut suffices, and no one has tried to make E21/E31-class methods cheap, only precise. (2) Bias floors already measured are near the 1k bar: E31's low-input closure at an exact skeleton is -.13 to -.45 pp aggregate (3 models, cost 4-5 FPE per skeleton); E21's observer is .6 pp on 12 copies; rank_exact is .9 pp on the target. (3) The blind region is nearly model-independent (contribution .58-.91 pp, sd ~.12), so it is not where the budget goes.

*Against.* (1) The across-skeleton variance is the binding constraint, not the low inputs: with the top-3 fixed, integrating out the low inputs cuts variance only 1.6x (E31), so 90-97% of the variance is across the 720 triples x 3-D values (E42). A deductive estimator must integrate a step-like function (E[q(1-q)] = .016-.028) over that 3-D space per triple with ~1 evaluation per triple at 1k FPE. Every tried rule is either inductive (Sobol, 3.5-4x over MC, E42) or coarse frozen classes with >= .9 pp bias on clean seeds and 2-17 pp on weak ones (T274b, T282). (2) The weak retrains carry the panel variance, and the only closure that is cheap (E31 lin) has per-skeleton errors of 2-2.5 pp that cancel on 3 models by luck; E21 reports its bias growing as accuracy falls. (3) The band contribution itself varies 3.2-6.2 pp across the zoo seeds (T269), so "the band is alike across models" holds only relative to the tail. No deductive method has ever been scored on more than 5 retrains.

My read: possible but unlikely (~15%), and decidable cheaply (D).

**(ii) 16k and above.** No (~3%). Bars are .21/.11/.054 pp; every deductive family has alpha ~ 0 (T271) with floors >= .3 pp; the only alpha > .5 family is mass-ordered exact enumeration, whose cost is the region count (>= 1e10). The prefactor collapse (T277: ~1000x in MSE ratio from T=3 to 10 at an unchanged rate) says the band integrand's variance relative to p(1-p) is what grows with T, and the tie-wall fingerprint (T239, T272, E23: in-band gate flips end the bands; w has no compact form) says the band cannot be summarised below the region level.

**Lower bound?** None exists, and none is near. These are empirical facts about one function class; they bound no estimator's bias. A worst-case statement would be CAPP-type hardness, out of reach. What would refute the pessimism: T270's g_1e-7(5->6) <= 7 and falling (exact route reopens at its own huge cost); the D-test's oracle floor <= .4 pp across 80 retrains (cheap regime reopens); any deductive family with alpha > .5 at T=10 on seeds.

## C. Designs for the cheap regime (RMS <= .85 pp at <= 6e6 FLOPs)

Order is by my odds. C4, C5 and C2 are included because the brief asked; I argue they should not get compute.

**C1. OBS-cheap: the observer family at its own cost, with a resolution knob.** *Computation.* For each of the 720 top-3 position triples and each tie kind (2~3, 1~2): one constructed anchor input (model-free conditional medians), one forward pass (gate pattern), reverse passes for the two strongest rival margins (2 FPE), closed-form mean/variance of each margin when the 7 low inputs are i.i.d. truncated normals below M3 and (M1, M2) vary within the anchor's cell (model-free moments, cached), P(error | cell) = Phi(-mu/sigma) or a bivariate Phi for two rivals, summed over the model-free cell masses. Cone clipping (E21 v4) only along the gap axis, from the Jacobian rows of the comparator neurons (no extra passes). Knob: anchors per triple (gap bins x value bins). Cost: ~1.4k anchors x 3-4 FPE ~ 5k FPE at the coarsest useful setting; 15-25k with E21's gap bins. *Why it might reach the bar.* E21 v4 is the only deductive estimator with RMS ~.6 pp on 12 models; its 215k cost was 17,280 anchors x 10.5 passes (full Jacobian for all 160 pre-activations). Replacing the full Jacobian by two reverse passes plus comparator-row clipping cuts cost ~20x; T269's exact 3-D integration (nodes in log gap) replaces E21's 1728-node grids. *Decisive test.* Run E21's existing `est.py` at (1,1,1), (1,1,2), (2,2,2) on the 80 DEV2 retrains; record e_m and C_m; compute F per config and the per-model bias against accuracy. Then the cheapened version. *Stop rule.* Every config has F > 30 on DEV2, or bias grows with falling accuracy at slope > 10% (weak models under-served). *Go.* Some config F <= 3 (point) -> freeze, submit to T281 for the HP2 curve. *Reuses.* observer/core.py, est.py; gap_formula's quadrature; T269 profile code for the bias-vs-accuracy diagnostic.

**C2. Exact k=3 prefix masses + observer tail.** 792 exact prefix regions with closed-form 3-D cone masses (cheap, model-specific), then C1 conditioned on each region's canonical point. Cost ~792 x 5 FPE ~ 4k. *Why it probably fails.* T274b/T282: freezing early steps and running late steps exactly leaves .2-.65 pp (m = 6) and freezing *late* steps is the whole bias (m <= 4 worse than m = 0). The decisive gates are the comparator flips at the readout, not the prefix. Exact prefix structure buys nothing for the error. *Test.* None needed beyond T274b; mark not funded unless C1 shows a prefix-dependent bias.

**C3. BLIND-11: a deductive blind-region rate.** The blind region (2nd largest <= 0, mass 11/1024 exactly) has 11 cases: no positive input (1/1024) and one positive at position q (10/1024). Per case, one canonical affine map + the truncated-normal closure for the 9 negative inputs gives P(correct | case); 11 forwards + 11 maps ~ 100 FPE. *Why.* Blind contributions span .58-.91 pp across 8 models; a fixed 8/9 rate costs up to .36 pp; the required precision is only ~.15 pp. *Test.* Predicted vs sampled P(err | blind) on DEV2 (diagnostic sampling). *Stop.* RMS > .25 pp of contribution. A component for every other design, not an estimator.

**C4. Presumption of independence over ranks 4-10 given the top-3.** This is E31's `lin` closure (4-5 FPE per skeleton, -.13 to -.45 pp aggregate on 3 models) and the low-input half of C1. As a separate design it does not fit the budget: a faithful skeleton integral needs >= 720 triples x several value nodes, and any cheaper treatment of (M1, M2, M3) collapses to frozen classes (rank_exact, >= .9 pp). Fund only as the oracle part of D.

**C5. Winer's random-init method, extended.** ARC's matching results for random models are cumulant propagation (S8) and deduction-projection (S10, S11); both rest on randomness making correlations small. On M_16,T the faithful port misses by 41-100 pp (E35), because a scalar input per step makes the step-1 units perfectly correlated. The extension to trained nets is "advice": a reference network plus a residual law. T37-P1 (independent residual laws off by 22-48 pp) and T241's hybrid (+.22 pp, fitted, failing in the last 2-3 steps) say the residual integral *is* the original problem. Do not fund; the right use of Winer's work is the T203 comparison questions.

**C6. ANAT-80: what makes a retrain weak.** Not an estimator; the diagnostic the panel change demands. On the 80 DEV2 models (2e6 samples each, ~20 CPU-min), decompose error into blind / 2~3 band / 1~2 band / soft tail by triple and x9 case; count "gross-error triples" (error rate > 20% at gap >= .2); regress accuracy on these parts. *Why.* If weak models are weak through a few dozen gross-error triples, a 720-point canonical census (720 FPE, each answer an exact computation) plus the affine-map mass of the wrong half-space per flagged triple is a 1k-FPE deductive estimator of the model-specific part, with the band treated by C1 at low resolution. If weakness is diffuse band widening, only C1 can work. *Stop.* Nothing to stop; it decides between C1-low-res and C1-plus-census. *Reuses.* gap_formula/profile.py, tailcheck.py.

## D. Start immediately: the oracle bias floor on DEV2 (pre-registered)

1. DEV2 truths (open; compute 2e7-sample truths if hp2/dev2/truths.json is still missing; SE 5e-5).
2. Oracle floor: `stoch_margin/diag_cond.py` lin reduction, 20k sampled skeletons x 256 low draws per model (diagnostic sampling only), on all 80 models -> per-model aggregate bias b_m. Also E21 `est.py` at (1,1,1) and (1,1,2) -> e_m, C_m, F.
3. ANAT-80 from the same samples.

**Go line (written before data):** G1: RMS_m(b_m) <= .40 pp over the 80 retrains, with |mean| <= .25 pp and no trend in b_m against accuracy steeper than .05 pp per pp. Then the low-input closure is good enough for a 4k-FPE deductive estimator and the work becomes deterministic skeleton integration (C1). G2: any E21 config has point F <= 3 on DEV2 -> freeze it and submit to T281 for the HP2 curve. **Dead:** RMS_m(b_m) > .80 pp -> the frozen-gate/closure family is dead in the cheap regime (scoped to lin/observer closures), and the only deductive route left is T270's exact decision. Cost: ~3-5 CPU-hours, all code exists. Report the bias split by blind / band / tail per model so the failure, if it comes, names its part.

## E. Odds and top 3

Odds (subjective). A deductive estimator passes the strict own-cost bar at 1k-4k FPE on HP2 within 3 months: **15%** (30% if G1 passes, < 3% if it fails). At 16k+: **3%**. Lenient (F <= 10) somewhere on the curve: **40%**. Anyone, strict, within a year: **10-15%**. That ARC's reported solution meets the strict own-cost bar on retrains: **20-30%**; meets ARC's own "roughly match" (lenient): **60%**. My guess at its shape: a rank-class x presumption-of-independence estimator (their M_4,3 A) at ~1-2 pp RMS and ~1e4 FPE, which is F ~ 20-50 and would pass only their lenient reading.

Top 3: **(1) the D-test** (decides the cheap regime for ~5 CPU-hours; its ANAT-80 half is needed regardless, because the panel is now mostly models we have never dissected). **(2) T270 k=6 masses** (running; the one route with a clean win condition). **(3) C1 at its coarsest setting on DEV2, only if G1 passes**; otherwise spend the effort on the T203 package, since under own-cost scoring the comparison with ARC will turn on accounting conventions (what is charged, what counts as sampling), and those must be fixed before we see their method.
