## Results
The anatomy wrapper passes a terminal activation cone to `_cell_error`, which
clips competing output margins and returns its error contribution. The wrapper
then separately integrates the whole activation cone. Consequently `err` and
`leaf_mass-err` can both be positive for one archive index. `rays` stores only
`len(c.R)`. Neither a kind tag nor a representative point proves uniform
correctness. The tolerance-based wrong tag and uncharged diagnostic leaf mass
are accurately described. The pinned GPU collector has separate error and
optional whole-leaf buffers; the export uses the former with deterministic
stride selection and H8T5, not a complete H16T5 leaf corpus.

For nonnegative class contributions, the fixed-subset conditional ratio is
monotone increasing in its selected sum and decreasing in its complement.
The author's lower/upper interval ratios follow immediately. A subset meeting
the lower ratio certifies an upper bound on the required count; the sum of the
k largest individual upper bounds below q times a positive total lower bound
excludes every k-subset. The completeness and unknown-mass qualifications are
essential. Sorting uncertain point masses does not certify optimality.

Subdivision of a constant-label region into equal-mass pieces changes its
coverage count to ceil(q*n) while preserving the function. Thus the specified
partition statistic can test an operational hypothesis but is not an intrinsic
representation or computational lower bound. Conditional versus absolute
omitted mass, rare-class error amplification, changing error sets across models,
and non-iid cell populations are correctly separated.

Raw gaps vary radially, normalized gaps can vary directionally, and an input
gap bin requires intersection probabilities or a verified homogeneous-membership
certificate. Blind-event mass (T+1)/2^T uses independent symmetric signs and the
actual sequence length. Prefix evidence alone does not establish final accuracy.
