# Red-team of the T317c candidate (H-L1) before any contact with ARC

Referee: Fable, 2026-10-18. Read-only. Sources: dossier @77738944 (claude/t241-neuron-account), r1_cont code @5b4d4806 and its R1W/R0 lineage (claude/t218-tie-tail-labels), scorer README/score.py/cost_t317.py/results (claude/hp1-scorer @67e57f00), INDEX/TASKS/Hilton notes (ccr-6a61f25c-wn80ii), panel manifests and capacity_ceiling README (claude/t192-gpu-cost-law). One piece of arithmetic on the open DEV2 results (`results_d.json` est_c vs `dev2/truths.json`); no estimator was run.

**Verdict in one line.** The measured statement "F = 5-7 on sealed retrains under our FLOP convention" is sound and well audited. The claim as worded in H-L1 ("roughly matches plain sampling", "deductive") would not survive ARC: the method is 5-7x *worse* than sampling, its accuracy rests on approximations validated only by sampling over development models, and it fails on the weight-noise half of ARC's stated model distribution. No fatal flaw; eight major issues of substance or framing.

## Ranked issues

**1. [major, framing] The claim overstates.** ARC's word is "match" (Hilton 33:12); H-L1 says "roughly matches". F = 5.08 (HP3) / 6.76 (HP2) means sampling at the same compute has 5-7x lower MSE; equivalently the estimator is worth n* = 1,069 / 812 samples and costs 5.4k. The 10x "lenient" bar is a project convention read out of E32 (ARC's M_4,3 estimators at 2.4-10x under one op-count), and ARC's own "roughly match" text was for ~2x RMSE. The talk notes themselves say the lenient bar "rests only on the M_4,3 blog wording". *Fix:* never say "match"; state F, n*, cost and the direction (worse).

**2. [major, substance] "Deductive" describes the runtime, not the justification.** T318 measured the exact walk at 0.72 pp RMS (+0.55 mean) from a Monte Carlo of closure c, versus a closure floor of 0.47 pp (−0.16 mean): roughly 80% of the 0.8 pp error, and the whole bias, comes from the declared approximations (one transverse slice per region covering ~99% of the input mass, product of two 1-D correct sets, blind g12 assumed correct, tmax = 0.5 cut, μ-linearised lows). Every one of these was *chosen* by sampling on DEV2: closure c among four by 200k paired skeletons per model (T304 Stage A), tmax by a scan (T302), the dropped blind walk by cost with its effect measured by MC (T304 V3), G by a convergence check. No bound is derived from the weights for any of them (T319's attempt failed). The noisy-copy failure (HP1 abs σ .05: bias +6.6 pp, F 206) shows the approximations encode training-family regularities, not properties read off the weights, and ARC named weight noise as an equally valid model distribution. Under Hilton's test ("assume other things will behave similarly"), a scheme whose validity is established by sampling across models is at least "a little bit cheating"; the precedent in our favour is ARC's own M_4,3 estimator A with its tuned q = 0.2 (E32). The two net biases also partly cancel (−0.16 + 0.55 → +0.44), which is fragile. *Fix:* label the method "sampling-free at runtime; approximation scheme selected and validated by Monte Carlo on 80 development retrains"; publish the per-approximation error budget on a sealed panel (the dossier lists it as open).

**3. [major, embarrassment risk] The scored code contains a Monte Carlo.** `r1w2._regions` draws 1,000,000 × 10 standard normals with `default_rng(0)` at import to set the transverse points, and `gauss_v4`/`_gl`/`ndtr(nodes)` are recomputed inside every `est` call (~700 FPE of uncharged model-free work per call, +13% of C, by my count). The constants are model-free, so this is not sampling the model, but ARC's written heuristic for "mechanistic" is "avoids any random or pseudorandom sampling" (S7), and a reader who greps for `random` finds it. T317d replaces it with exact quadrature, but (a) T317d has not been scored on HP3, (b) its estimates differ by up to .0115 pp and it failed its own .01 pp gate (DRI accepted), and (c) INDEX H-L1 says the gaps are "closed in T317d" next to T317c's score, which invites the reading that the confirmed number belongs to the clean code. T317d's 2 × 800k-point trapezoid grid (~1e8 FLOPs ≈ 20k FPE once) is also gratuitously large for a 1-D integral; if anyone charges precomputation per model it dwarfs C. *Fix:* present T317d only, after its HP3 score, with the constants computed by a quadrature cheap enough to charge in full (< 100 FPE); until then disclose the seeded MC in one sentence.

**4. [major, metric] Cost latitude is real; the verdict survives it on HP3, not necessarily on HP2.** Convention choices and their effect: ARC-consistent sampling side (5,948 = forward + ReLU + argmax + label, RNG excluded, as in ARC's S9 rules: fine); correct implementation latitude +9% (T320's direction-testing roots); every executed ndtr +4%; uncharged per-call tables +13% (item 3). Stacked, C ≈ 6.8k: HP3 F ≈ 6.4 (upper CI ≈ 8.9, still a pass); HP2 F ≈ 8.5 (upper ≈ 11, loses "candidate"). Wall-clock is the other reading of "amount of computation": the Python walk takes ~0.75 s per model against ~10 ms for 5.4k vectorised forwards (~100x), because FLOP counts treat the data-dependent control flow (argmin, unique, where, merge) as free bookkeeping. ARC counts ops/FLOPs (S4, S9, flopscope), so this is presentation, not scoring. *Fix:* report C as a 5.3-6.8k range with each convention, state the FLOP-vs-wall-clock gap, and keep "candidate" tied to HP3.

**5. [major, metric] The denominator is the panel's p(1−p), not the target's.** Panel mean accuracy is ~0.92 (p(1−p) 0.069-0.0725); M_16,10 is 0.953 (0.045), near the top of the retrain distribution (capacity_ceiling: a "lucky draw"). At the same MSE a target-like denominator gives F 7.7 (DEV2), 7.9 (HP3), 10.5 (HP2). Counter-evidence, on development data only: the error shrinks with accuracy (DEV2 slope −0.067 pp per pp; on the 15 models with p > 0.95: bias +0.01 pp, RMS 0.46 pp, F 2.7). And the dossier never states est(M_16,10) against truth.txt (0.95318), the first number ARC will ask for; only the 6-model HP1-clean aggregate is given. *Fix:* report est(target); ask the scorer for HP2/HP3 F on the p > 0.94 subset and the error-vs-p slope (aggregates reveal no sealed truths).

**6. [major, selection] Selected on HP2, confirmed on HP3; say so.** ~30 estimate variants on DEV2; five lineage submissions to HP2 (T300, T306, T317, T317b, T317c) plus a scorer recount that moved T317b's upper CI from 10.07 to 10.25. The commit log shows T317b's HP2 score at 10:46 UTC and the T317c pre-registration plus commit at 10:50: the cost savings were conceived after the near miss, and the stopping rule ("stop when upper CI ≤ 10") was conditioned on HP2. That does not bias F (estimates unchanged, cost real) but it does make "candidate on HP2" a constructed threshold. HP3 is clean for T317c's F given that T317c was selected; residual: same recipe, same data seed 41 and same creator (T192) as HP2/DEV2, and HP3 was commissioned because HP2 passed. Panel-to-panel spread (4.7 / 6.8 / 5.1) is ±30%; pooled sealed F over 200 models ≈ 5.9. *Fix:* the sentence "selected on HP2 after five submissions of the lineage; confirmed once on a fresh panel" belongs in the claim.

**7. [major, framing] The bar is nearly reachable by a constant, so lead with the information content.** Under the project's own rule (1k-FPE floor) the panel-mean constant scores F ≈ 13-14 (n* ≈ 73 FPE). T317c's n* is 1,069-1,147: 11-16x the prior's information, corr(est, truth) = 0.978 and R² = 0.94 on DEV2. That is the genuine positive result and it is more defensible than "within 10x of sampling". Bias² is 27-31% of MSE; an oracle de-biasing would give F ≈ 3.3, and the dossier rightly refuses to fit it.

**8. [minor-major, process] The scoring rule moved to where the method passes.** Directive 7 (seeds + noisy panels) → amendment 1 (fixed budgets 16k-256k, all groups) → amendment 2 (own-cost F, retrains primary). T317c fails the first two. ARC's phrasing supports own-cost, but the history must be disclosed. Also disclose: the dossier author also performed both "independent" checks (T312, T320); "CONFIRMED" is an internal label and should read "verified internally (second implementation, independent scorer)".

**Minor:** `walk_cont(max_cells=400)` truncates silently if hit (undeclared; probably never hit at tmax .5); percentile bootstrap on a ratio with Ce not resampled (negligible); the T318 0.72 pp figure is on 10 models; INDEX H-L1's "every step derived from the weights or a declared approximation" should drop "derived from the weights" for the approximations; the whole T317→HP3 chain ran in 41 minutes on one day, which is fine but should be visible in the pre-registration commit order.

## Recommended wording

"On 100 fresh retrains of AlgZoo's M_16,10 training recipe (sealed panel, frozen code, scored once), a sampling-free estimator (exact piecewise-linear ReLU-gate walks along the two gap directions of each position triple, combined under five declared approximations whose validity was established by Monte Carlo on 80 separate development retrains) estimates each model's accuracy with RMS error 0.81 pp (bias +0.42 pp; corr with truth ≈ 0.98) at a counted cost of 5.4k forward-pass equivalents (5.3-6.8k under alternative FLOP conventions). Plain sampling at the same compute would have about 5x lower MSE (F = 5.1, 95% CI 3.4-7.0; 6.8 [4.9, 8.8] on a second sealed panel used for earlier submissions of the same lineage). The estimator does not match sampling and fails on weight-noised copies (bias up to +6.6 pp). It uses no random inputs to the model; the scored version sets two model-free constants by a seeded Monte Carlo over the input law, replaced by exact quadrature in a successor not yet scored."

## Share now, after fixes, or not at all?

Not now, and not as a candidate solution: under the owner's own rule ("unless we have a candidate solution, we should not contact ARC") this is not one. It fails "match" by 5x and fails half of ARC's model distribution. After the fixes (T317d scored on HP3; est(target); high-accuracy-subset F; per-approximation error budget; cost range; the wording above) it is a credible *progress note* that could accompany the protocol questions in T203(d), provided it is framed as "here is how far a sampling-free surrogate gets on retrains and why it stops there", with the inductive QMC reference (v5q, F ≈ 1 at 4k) shown alongside so ARC sees we know the difference.
