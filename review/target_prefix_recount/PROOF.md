# Independent exact prefix geometry

This implementation counts full-dimensional **gate-history regions**, matching the intended T192 prefix notion, not the minimal number of affine output maps. It uses no author cone routines or artificial fan. Different histories can have equal eventual hidden maps; they are still distinct gate-history regions. A count through k4 cannot itself prove exponential growth through T10.

## Exact model and recurrence

Let every stored dyadic weight equal its integer coefficient divided by one common positive D. At depth t the hidden map has integer coefficient matrix M divided by D^t. At the next step the new preactivation row is W_h(integer)*M plus W_i(integer)*D^t in the fresh coordinate. Thus all decisions can use exact integer signs. Zero coefficients produce globally zero gate forms and stay OFF. Floating author matrix products/tolerances are a different arithmetic model; agreement or discrepancy must retain this distinction.

Wi must have at least one nonzero entry. Initially x0 positive/negative gives two pointed half-lines and their exact masks. For an all-zero input vector the entire zero-state network is a separate trivial case; this first API explicitly rejects it rather than introducing duplicate artificial half-lines.

## Extrusion without an artificial fan

Suppose C in R^d is pointed and full-dimensional, represented by complete extreme rays r and constraints n.x>=0. Its extension C x R has lineality in the fresh coordinate. Choose the first neuron whose fresh coefficient a=Wi_j*D^t is nonzero. For its form v=(b,a), project each old ray to the boundary using

    r'=(|a| r, -sign(a) b.r).

These directions have v.r'=0 and preserve the old conic coordinates up to positive scaling. The ON half is generated by all r' and sign(a)e_(d+1); the OFF half uses all r' and -sign(a)e_(d+1). Decomposing any point into its boundary projection and signed vertical remainder proves equality with the respective intersection. Each side is pointed and full-dimensional. Their interiors are disjoint and together cover the extrusion up to the gate boundary.

## Exact pointed clipping

For a full-dimensional pointed polyhedral cone with complete extreme rays and a valid complete constraint representation, two distinct extreme rays are adjacent exactly when the normals active on both have rank d-2. Redundant/dependent constraints do not change this rank criterion. With v.p>0 and v.n<0 on adjacent rays, the crossing ray is

    (-v.n) p + (v.p) n.

The clipped extreme rays are inherited rays on the retained side or boundary plus these crossings. All integer ray/normal normalizations divide by positive gcd only. No sign reversal, epsilon threshold or dropping thin cones is permitted. A branch with no strictly positive (or negative) ray is boundary-only and omitted; a globally zero form is assigned OFF without clipping.

Each branch appends one gate sign to the history. Gates at the same recurrent step depend on the fixed preactivation matrix from the previous state, so choosing the nonzero-input pivot before other neurons changes only enumeration order. A fixed history defines an intersection of linear halfspaces and hence a convex cone. Starting without an artificial fan gives one cone per feasible full-dimensional history, not multiple pieces that must later be deduplicated.

## Positive-volume witnesses and invariants

Cone checks establish full ray/normal rank, feasibility of every ray and extremality by rank d-1 of its active normals. Completeness follows inductively from extrusion and edge clipping, not from checking a finite set of witnesses. The sum of all extreme rays lies strictly inside every nonzero supporting halfspace of a full-dimensional cone. Its positive primitive normalization supplies an exact witness. Reconstructing all historical gate forms at that witness verifies strict signs for every nonzero form and OFF for coefficient-zero forms. Strict finite inequalities imply a nonempty open neighborhood and positive Gaussian measure; boundary-only histories are not counted.

No exact arithmetic makes a clipping algorithm complete without these representation invariants. The source/analytic controls and independently reconstructed saved certificates remain necessary. Integer operation counts are a partial named ledger; gcd, comparisons, hashing, power, control and bit complexity remain separately qualified.
