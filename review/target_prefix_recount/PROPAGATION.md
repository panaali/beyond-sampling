# Scope of extrapolating gate-history counts

Suppose Wi has a nonzero entry, the initial state is zero and the recurrence is bias-free as in this task. Let N_t count full-dimensional histories of all gate signs through depth t. Every such cone C extrudes to C x R when the fresh input is introduced. The gate with a nonzero fresh coefficient takes both strict signs on nonempty open subsets of this extrusion. Each subset has full dimension and carries a different extended history. The remaining gates subdivide without discarding either full-dimensional subset. Therefore

    N_(t+1) >= 2 N_t.

This is a lower bound on gate-history counts only. Distinct histories may share affine maps, equivalent later behavior or an aggregatable representation. For a verified depth4 count, the consequent bound is N10>=64*N4. Empirically observed multipliers near23,27 or30 do not by themselves imply that future multipliers remain that large. This corollary provides no algorithmic lower bound, required mass computation, Gaussian probability statement or claim that exact estimation is impractical. It does not rule out region aggregation.
