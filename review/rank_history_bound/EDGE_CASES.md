# Written edge cases; no executable controls

1. **Reset and boundary ownership.** With A=0 and w=(1,-1), u>0 gives ON/OFF, u<0 gives OFF/ON, and u=0 gives OFF/OFF. There are two positive-volume words and three words if the boundary is incorrectly counted. This is why the rank-zero case is separate from B(H,0)=1.

2. **Identically zero input and state.** A=0,w=0 gives one all-OFF child throughout the parent cylinder; strict positivity of a zero form is never required.

3. **Rank-one sharpness.** Let h=s v with s>0 and a fixed nonzero nonnegative v. Choose nonzero w_i and distinct thresholds -(Wv)_i/w_i. The section s=1 has H distinct points on its new-input line and exactly H+1 open intervals, attaining B(H,1). The image rank alone does not eliminate width dependence.

4. **Rank-two section intersection.** Let H=2, C=R_+^2, A=I, W=I and w=(-1,-1). In the section h1+h2=1,h1,h2>0, the two lines u=h1 and u=h2 cross at (h1,u)=(1/2,1/2). The four open sign combinations are realized, attaining B(2,2)=4. This is an analytic one-step construction, not a claim about a particular trained parent's recurrence history.

5. **Coincident/zero forms.** Repeated same-sign affine forms cannot produce independent flips; a form identically zero on L×R is fixed OFF. Neither creates extra chambers. Opposite forms can produce a new word on their common zero set, which remains boundary-only unless both forms vanish identically.

6. **The positive image matters.** If the nonnegative-state assumption is dropped, take h=x in R, with next forms x+u and x−u on the full plane. There are four open words, exceeding B(2,1)=3. No single positive h-section covers both signs. This is outside the ReLU hidden-state theorem and prevents extending it to arbitrary signed linear states without a different bound.

7. **Parent dimension matters.** For A=I in R² but C a single nonnegative ray, the matrix rank is two while dim span A(C) is one. Such C is not a full-dimensional parent in R² and contributes no ambient positive-volume history. Relative-domain counting requires identifying the relative input/image dimension; it cannot silently reuse full-dimensional completeness or probability claims.

8. **Discarded directions.** For an abstract parent C=R×R_+ and A(x1,x2)=x2 v with v nonnegative and nonzero, the parent has lineality, but the image is positive and rank one. The section argument still works because it discards the kernel direction. No claim that this abstract C is generated by a particular nonzero-input RNN is needed.

All cases are written algebra under the theorem's stated scope. No matrices were evaluated, ranks computed, histories enumerated or target observations used. They are independently reviewable counterexamples/domain checks, not an empirical test suite.
