## Results
1. Coverage of correct/error contributions is defined for complete disjoint
   leaves and positive class totals; a mixed leaf can enter both lists. At
   equal q the omitted *fractions* match while absolute masses differ. Such
   counts are not cells per unit mass and are not invariant to subdivision.
2. For unit u, distance to xi=xj is |ui-uj|/sqrt(2), and spherical angular
   distance is its arcsine. Taking the smaller of the two relevant adjacent
   order gaps preserves this distinction; fixed numerical thresholds should
   not silently be converted. This is not a claim about actual computed bins.
3. A mean of unit rays depends on the represented ray list: repeating e1 in
   (e1,e2) changes its normalized direction from (1,1) to (2,1). A redundant
   asymmetric interior ray can also change the center without changing the
   cone. Fixed complete extreme-ray lists can remove that ambiguity, but a
   representative still does not establish whole-leaf gap membership.
4. Whole-leaf mass assigned by a representative defines a diagnostic bin
   statistic. It differs from the probability of the leaf intersected with
   the true gap event. Blind classification needs whole-domain ownership or
   the same explicit representative qualifier. Overlap and zero denominators
   must be declared; the omitted middle gap interval is not a normalization
   defect if it is intentionally outside the contrast.
5. The ratio of far/near densities alone cannot establish both near-density
   closeness and an absolute far-density reduction: multiplying both trained
   densities by a common positive factor leaves the ratio unchanged. Prefix
   controls have no correctness labels, so they cannot directly identify the
   complexity of a final error set.
6. Source decision thresholds are retained. The width quantifiers of growth
   and global disproof, zero denominators and pooling of distinct random
   controls need clarification before a single combined conclusion. Different
   trained T3/T4 checkpoints are a cross-checkpoint comparison, not successive
   prefixes of one frozen network. One-seed comparisons do not imply a
   universal training or architecture claim.
