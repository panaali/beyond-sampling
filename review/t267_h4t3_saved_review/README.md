# T267 H4T3 independent saved review

## Question
Do the fixed H4T3 saved cone incidences, correctness certificates, facet-chain reduction, scalar integration records and report arithmetic reconcile?

## Method
`check.py` uses direct three-dimensional determinants and cross products, exact Fraction constraints, independently reconstructed facet coefficients, and per-term dyadic rounding. It checks every gate and correct cone's ray/active-plane incidences, full-dimensional rank witness, and contained simplices. Saved terminal affine maps and readout weights bind output-margin normals; rank-plane IDs and orientations identify exactly one larger input, with permanent output ties assigned to the earlier class. No recurrent-map generation or model forward is performed.

The chain reconstruction checks original normalized determinants, each anchor orientation, facet multiplicities, retained terms, source identity records, dyadic totals and named operation counts. Geometry-engine internal work counters are source-qualified except the gate/correct-cone/simplex totals reconstructed here. Reported maximum bit counts are instrumentation, not independent complexity bounds.

The scalar check reconstructs normalized vertices, dot products, square roots, the positive denominator, and arithmetic from the saved angle to unsigned/signed contribution. It does **not** call `atan2`, the author's mass function, a model, geometry capture or a random generator. The six controls have exact rational mass targets; other `atan2` returns remain unverified libm premises. All progress records, sums, cost-category conventions and reported differences are reconciled.

## Status
Source frozen before reading completed numerical output files. The author's brief completion summary arrived during implementation; neither scope nor thresholds were selected from its numbers. A distinct source/resource gate and successfully published immutable completed-input binding precede the sole saved run.

## Compute accounting
Saved exact/scalar contractions only. No author imports, recurrence, new model inputs, capture, integration kernel, `atan2`, RNG or quadrature. Partial review state and traceback are preserved on failure. No output overwrite, adaptive tolerance change or automatic retry.

## Claims and limits
Passing supports consistency of these retained artifacts. It does not establish global completeness/disjointness of the inherited geometry, recurrence-map correctness, historical checkpoint identity, native floating error bounds, full machine cost, or general boundary-method performance. Correct-cone inequalities certify their represented rays and positive-hull implications; they do not prove missing cones absent. Model dyadics are retained-output/source-bound rather than independently decoded from the NPZ. Integration totals remain numerical, and discovery/matching/recording cost remains charged separately.

## Alternatives not ruled out
Different triangulations or anchors may reveal additional cancellation. A smaller term count can still cost more after discovery. Dimension- and checkpoint-specific observations do not transfer to H16T10 or sealed challenge evaluation.

## Failure modes
Malformed pins/schema, zero/duplicate rays, incidence or orientation disagreement, wrong permanent-tie ownership, scalar arithmetic mismatch or resource exhaustion remains a preserved failed review. No new geometry or mass evaluation is authorized to repair it.
