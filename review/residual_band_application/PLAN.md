## Zero, first and second boundary corrections

For each band, let c be its standard-normal endpoint nearer0, d=far−c and orientation q=sign(d). Its positive Gaussian mass is `q[Phi(c+d)−Phi(c)]`. Define

- zero correction: retain the frozen error only;
- first correction: add residual_sign times `q*phi(c)*d`;
- second correction: add residual_sign times `q*phi(c)*(d−c*d*d/2)`;
- direct diagnostic: use the signed exact-CDF difference on the same bands.

The absolute per-band ideal-arithmetic remainders are bounded by `phi(1)*d²/2` and `phi(0)*|d|³/6`, since `sup|phi'|=phi(1)` and `sup|phi''|=phi(0)`. Sum absolute bounds with positive outer weights. These are inner Taylor bounds only: they do not enclose floating evaluation, face-law approximation or outer quadrature error. Wide second-order bands can give negative approximations; retain them without repair.
