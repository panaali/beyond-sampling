## Independent algebra check

For centered row-state matrix H and a real projector P, set V=H P^T. The squared projected-norm ratio is E=||V||²/||H||². Expanding the residual norm gives the reduction D=(||H||²-||H-V||²)/||H||²=(2<H,V>-||V||²)/||H||². Thus E=D precisely when <H,V>=||V||² for these data; orthogonality of P guarantees that property for all H, while general idempotence does not.

The supplied P=[[1,1],[0,0]] is idempotent. For centered rows(0,1),(0,-1), projected rows are(1,0),(-1,0), and residual rows(-1,1),(1,-1). Hence E=1 while D=-1. R=[[1,-1],[0,1]] has inverse[[1,1],[0,1]], and the first eigen-coordinate projector R[:,0]R^-1[0,:] is exactly P. A=R diag(1,2)R^-1=[[1,-1],[0,2]] realizes this complete real eigensystem. The centered rows may equally arise from strictly positive states(1,1),(1,3), so positivity of original state coordinates does not repair the identity. Replacing the off-diagonal entry by k yields E=k²; large values need not originate in numerical failure. No target value of k, conditioning or energy was calculated.

The source expression in phaseA_modes.py is E, not D. It is legitimately a projected squared-norm ratio and may be descriptive, but it is not an additive partition of variance or variance removed. Resampling a component has a further cross-term/donor interpretation and is not identical to setting that component to zero. A nonzero centered denominator is also necessary. The author's explicit warning about ill-conditioned modes is useful, but does not make E a removed-variance share in the other patterns. Relabeling is justified by algebra; no empirical accuracy is overturned.
