## Alternatives not ruled out
An abstract proxy may be intentional; different conventions alone do not show an implementation error. Native kernels may execute fewer or more operations than scalar mathematical expressions. Existing T5 numerical accuracy, certificate completeness, diagnostic timing contamination and noisy-model coverage remain separate.

## Failure modes
Instrumentation can alter rounding or short-circuit order; fixed bitwise controls and source parity are mandatory. Opaque calls, missing historical layouts, integer/control/memory/setup and libm internals prevent a full numerator. Source agreement does not validate quadrature accuracy.
