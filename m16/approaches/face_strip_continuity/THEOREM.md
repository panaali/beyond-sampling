# Local crossing-face refinement

Fix the T155 axis interval s∈[a,b], target u=1 and input rank domain D=(L×U)∪(U×L), where L=[-6,1], U=[1,6]. The first input prefix is below u throughout both frozen intervals, so D is the relevant need1 domain and its Gaussian probability is 2LU in the prior notation.

For any two states on the interval, correctness can change only across one of the inherited extended affine boundaries. In an actual readout crossing, target logit equals a competitor and is at least every other competitor at the crossing. The occurrence's first/middle gate inequalities and final support inequalities also hold in their closed forms. At a final-gate crossing, retaining all first/middle/rank faces is conservative. Zero-area faces must be retained: they can carry an entire crossing curve as the state moves. A proportional boundary is removed only when every occurrence on both rank sides has empty closed face. Permanent support-identical ties have fixed ownership; transitions involving all-off support remain covered by final-gate boundaries.

For each boundary A*x+B*y+C*s+E=0 with (A,B) nonzero, solve for the input coordinate with larger absolute coefficient. Substituting this equality into every inequality is exact and yields closed affine halfspaces in (s,remaining input). Intersecting the bounded rectangle and all halfspaces preserves emptiness, lines and points. No floating tolerance is used. A zero input normal with C!=0 remains unresolved under the inherited guard.

Let q=min_{s∈[a,b]}(C*s+E)^2/[2(A²+B²)]. The minimum is zero if the endpoint offsets straddle zero, and otherwise occurs at the smaller absolute endpoint offset. For a standard two-dimensional Gaussian, the density of its projection normal to the boundary at any intermediate boundary position is at most phi(0)exp(-q) <= 1/(1+q). Integrating that one-dimensional density over the swept strip gives a bound of |C| |ds| / sqrt(A²+B²) times 1/(1+q), hence also the conservative rational bound |C| |ds| / max(|A|,|B|) times that factor. Restricting to D cannot increase this unconditional mass. Divide the sum by P(D) for the conditional pair value. The elementary inherited lower bound P(D)>2/81 gives prefix coefficient (81/2)|Wi_axis| times the sum. Negative proportional rescaling leaves all ratios and q unchanged.

The bounds describe direct pair-function variation across the frozen prefix intervals. They do not certify the older interpolation after the random first pair input, where the hidden state has mixed support. Neither input truncation error nor outer integration error is estimated here. Numeric displayed CDF normalizations are diagnostic; rational elementary bounds carry the stated real-arithmetic certificate.
