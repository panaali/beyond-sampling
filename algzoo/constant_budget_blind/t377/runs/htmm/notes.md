# Neuron labels for the second-largest-position RNN (32 neurons)

## Method
All access went through `probe.hidden` / `probe.predict` on Gaussian inputs (seeds 1, 2, 3, 7; never 20261016).
Predicted-vs-true accuracy of the model on seed 1: 0.993.
1. Screening (s1.py, s2.py): correlations and linear/ReLU fits of each h_10[i] against x_t, running maxima P_t,
   running second maxima Q_t, argmax/argsecond indicators.
2. Key observation (s5.py): to a very good approximation each neuron is relu(linear function of
   {x9, top-3 values of x0..x8, position of the largest/second-largest of x0..x8}). So the state after x8 holds
   the top-two values of the prefix plus a code for their positions, and the last step compares x9 against them.
3. Formula search (s3/s6/s11/s13.py): greedy forward selection (up to 4 features + bias = at most 5 fitted
   constants) of relu(b.features + c), fitted by Gauss-Newton on ReLU least squares, from a library: x_k, P8/Q8/R8
   (largest/second/third of x0..x8), P7, margins x_k-R8 and x_k-Q8, sums of |x| over early positions, and
   indicators "position of the largest (AM) / second-largest (A2) / either (T2) of x0..x8 lies in set S" with
   |S| <= 4. Hand-chosen simplifications for the clean neurons (s7.py). Final fit on 100k seed-2 inputs
   (final.py -> spec.json), tested on fresh seeds 3 and 7. labels.py is generated by gen.py.
   Discrete choices (feature selection and position sets) are declared in STRUCT.

## Overall picture
- Magnitude trackers (12, 5): ~a*P8 + b*Q8, always on, independent of x9.
- Top-two gap split by position (9, 26): 9 = 7.15*relu(x8 - max(x0..x7)); 26 = 6.68*relu(max(x0..x7) - max(Q7, x8)).
- x9 comparators: 22 = relu(x9 - P8) (x9 is the max), 31/19/18 = x9 beats Q8 (x9 is a top-two value),
  6 = relu(Q8 - x9) (x9 is not top-two); 1, 15, 8, 2, 20 = "x9 below Q8/P8" plus a distributed code for the
  position of the prefix maximum (each neuron a different subset of positions, like bits).
- Position slots: positive neurons for "x_k is among the top two of x0..x8" (27:x0, 25:x1/x3, 0:x2, 4:x4,
  13:x5, 7:x6, 16:x4 is max, 23:x7 is max) and negative ones "x_k is below the second-largest"
  (29:x1, 28:x3, 24:x7, 3:x8, 10:x8). Pure position codes of the argmax: 14, 11, 17, 30.
- The readout presumably combines: which positions hold the prefix top-two (slot neurons + position codes) and
  where x9 falls relative to P8 and Q8 (comparators) to pick the second-largest.
- Several slot neurons also carry a small baseline growing with sum |x0..x6|; neuron 21 is mostly this and
  poorly understood.
- Weak points: 11, 17, 21 (R^2 0.53-0.68) are position codes / baselines I could not compress into 5 constants;
  position-slot neurons sit at 0.76-0.88 because they mix codes for other positions' ranks.
